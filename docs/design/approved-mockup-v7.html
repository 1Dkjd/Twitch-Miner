What's real and what's invented
  • Your data: every campaign, reward, end date and minute count, and the 11 claimed rewards. Reward art is cropped from your screenshot, so it keeps Twitch's navy backdrop.
  • Favourite channels: example_streamer and another_favourite are made up. The add box suggests the real channel names from your campaigns (dnd, plus the AION 2 channels in your screenshot).
  • Invented: streamer names, the account name, claim times, the priority order (Palia, then Phasmophobia), the sign-in code, and every timestamp in the unhappy states.
  • The 10-reward stress test reuses AION 2 art with made-up minute marks. It only exists to show how a long campaign fits.
  • History's missed-reward rows are examples, collapsed at the bottom. You have no missed rewards.
  • Priorities and Settings controls work. "Mine now", "Link account" and the "change" channel link are static.
